# Public course workstream

## What this source is

A derived note about the `공개과정` workspace, based on the currently read planning, positioning, and operations documents for the premium public course.

## Why it matters

This source captures how a private teaching worldview is being turned into a public education product.
It is one of the clearest bridges between philosophy, offer design, operations, and participant-facing asset production.

## Key points

- the course targets working instructors, facilitators, and HRD practitioners
- the promise is to improve a real representative lecture, not just teach abstract teaching theory
- `design -> facilitation -> transfer + assetization` is the clearest recurrent structure in the current materials
- participant outputs, feedback records, AI assets, and SNS assets are meant to survive into future cohorts
- the course is positioned as premium and practice-heavy

## Tensions or gaps

- the currently read documents disagree on final duration: one key summary says 2 days, another says 3 days
- the source set shows rich launch preparation, but the public publishing URL is not yet captured in this wiki
