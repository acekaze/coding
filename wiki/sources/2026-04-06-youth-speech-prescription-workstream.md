# Youth speech prescription workstream

## What this source is

A derived note about the `청소년을 위한 말처방` workspace, based on the current research memo, deeper report, and the presence of the original adult book PDF.

## Why it matters

This source shows how an existing adult-oriented language and relationship framework is being translated into a youth-safe editorial form.
It is one of the strongest B2C expressions in the current corpus.

## Key points

- the adaptation is driven by adolescent-development research, not only tone preference
- rejection sensitivity is treated as a major editorial risk and design lens
- the work consistently favors scenarios, scripts, and concrete guidance over moralizing abstraction
- the desired tone is warm, autonomy-supportive, and careful about shame
- emotional independence is reframed so the book does not accidentally praise isolation

## Tensions or gaps

- the current wiki evidence is much stronger on research and adaptation logic than on actual rewritten chapters
- the source set points to the adult book as the substrate, but detailed chapter mapping is not yet represented here
