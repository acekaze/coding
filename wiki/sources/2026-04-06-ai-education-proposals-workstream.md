# AI education proposals workstream

## What this source is

A derived note about the `제안서` workspace, based on the integrated AI education proposal plus manager and practitioner approval-ready proposals.

## Why it matters

This source translates the broader education worldview into enterprise buying language.
It also reveals how the portfolio thinks about role-based AI education design.

## Key points

- the proposal system argues for splitting manager and practitioner education tracks
- manager education is framed as redesigning instruction, approval, and decision systems
- practitioner education is framed as redesigning daily execution workflows and quality control
- both tracks are tied to reusable templates, promptbooks, logs, and measurable operating outcomes
- the folder acts as both a sales workspace and an asset factory

## Tensions or gaps

- the current wiki ingest is based on three proposal documents and does not yet include the full slide or rendered deck corpus
- it is still unclear which proposal assets became delivered programs versus remaining internal sales assets
