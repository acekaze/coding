# Reusable education operations assets

## Summary

The current corpus repeatedly treats operations output as reusable educational infrastructure rather than as disposable delivery residue.

## Definition

This concept refers to organizing course work so that templates, prompts, participant outputs, 후기, decks, guides, and related artifacts can be reused, revised, and redeployed later.

## Why it matters

It explains why so many folders are filled with markdown files, slide assets, prompt structures, output folders, and cohort-oriented operations plans.
The work is being built to compound.

## Supporting ideas

- the public course operations draft explicitly separates planning, recruitment, operations, AI assets, SNS assets, outcomes, and next-cohort reuse
- the proposal workspace functions as both a sales surface and a template factory
- the instructional design workspace contains guides, scripts, decks, printable materials, and generated outputs in one system

## Tensions or caveats

- accumulation without curation can make later retrieval harder
- public-facing URLs are still thin compared with the richness of local assets
- reuse logic should not obscure which assets were exploratory versus actually deployed
