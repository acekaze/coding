# Dual-track AI education manager vs practitioner

## Summary

The proposal portfolio argues that AI education should be split by organizational leverage point, with managers and practitioners learning different operating behaviors.

## Definition

In this concept, manager education redesigns direction, approval, feedback, and decision systems, while practitioner education redesigns execution workflows, templates, and quality control.

## Why it matters

This is the clearest current strategic thesis inside the proposal corpus.
It prevents AI education from collapsing into one generic course for everyone.

## Supporting ideas

- manager materials emphasize 10-80-10, 1-3-1, 4-View, and 30-day operating routines
- practitioner materials emphasize grounding, CRAFT-O, templated outputs, and practical self-QA
- the integrated proposal still recommends separating the tracks

## Tensions or caveats

- organizations still need a coordination story between the two tracks
- role-based design requires approved tools, internal sample data, and operating follow-through
