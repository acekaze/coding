# Jeon Jong-mok

## Summary

Jeon Jong-mok is the central interpreting entity in the current corpus: an educator whose work spans B2B execution and B2C meaning-making through one shared worldview.

## What this entity is

A person-centered portfolio anchor represented in `C:/코딩/전종목`, with adjacent evidence in the public course, youth adaptation, proposal, and instructional design workstreams.

## Why it matters in this wiki

The surrounding projects make more sense when read as connected expressions of one stance:

- interpret experience before simplifying it
- turn insight into structure and action
- avoid empty performance language
- preserve dignity while remaining concrete

## Key facts

- the context file explicitly argues against reducing the work to speech training alone
- B2B language centers on communication, leadership, execution, collaboration, and AI questioning/design
- B2C language centers on self-language, meaning, loss, recovery, signature story, and human influence
- the portfolio logic appears to run from worldview to proposals, course design, public products, and editorial adaptation
- the source material also warns against overclaiming interpretive conclusions beyond direct evidence
