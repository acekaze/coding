# Andrej Karpathy

## Summary

Andrej Karpathy is the source author whose gist inspired this wiki workspace pattern.

## What this entity is

A researcher and educator associated here with the idea of using an LLM to maintain a persistent markdown wiki instead of relying only on repeated query-time retrieval from raw files.

## Why it matters in this wiki

This workspace is intentionally modeled after the operating pattern he described:

- raw sources stay immutable
- the wiki is the maintained knowledge layer
- the schema guides the LLM's maintenance behavior

## Key facts

- published a gist titled `LLM Wiki`
- framed the wiki as a compounding artifact
- emphasized ingest, query, and lint as recurring operations
