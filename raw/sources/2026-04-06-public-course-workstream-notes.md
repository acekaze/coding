## Core idea

This workstream is building a premium open course for working instructors.
The course is framed as redesigning a participant's real lecture rather than teaching generic speaking technique.

## Main evidence

- the execution summary frames the offer as a premium hands-on course for experienced trainers and instructors
- the decision document frames the course around `design -> facilitation -> transfer + assetization`
- the operations draft treats the course as a reusable asset system, not a one-off event
- AI and SNS are positioned as extensions of the instructor's teaching assets, not as separate trend topics

## Key themes

- real lecture improvement over abstract theory accumulation
- design-centered teaching rather than speech-performance coaching
- practice, feedback, and participant outputs at the center
- markdown-first operations and future cohort reuse

## Important tension

The currently read documents do not fully agree on final format:

- `공개과정_실행총괄_2026-05-01_02.md` describes a 2-day program on May 1-2, 2026 with a price of 1,100,000 KRW including VAT
- `교수법공개과정_결정본1차.md` describes the core positioning as a 3-day premium course

This looks like planning drift rather than a settled contradiction, so the wiki should keep the discrepancy visible.

## Why this matters in the broader corpus

This appears to be the public-product expression of the same worldview that also shows up in enterprise course design and proposal work:

- teaching is about interpretation and structure
- good learning products are redesigned through artifacts and feedback
- operations should leave reusable assets behind
