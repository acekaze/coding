## Core idea

The wiki should be used as an internal control tower for the larger `C:/코딩` portfolio rather than as a decorative archive.

## Operating loop

- each morning: re-orient, scan tracked projects, ingest only meaningful changes, and refresh the viewer if needed
- during the day: capture major decisions, reusable answers, and contradictions while they are fresh
- each week: lint the wiki for drift, duplication, and missing concepts

## Tracked default projects

- 공개과정
- 청소년을 위한 말처방
- 제안서

## Main rule

Source projects remain the execution layer.
The wiki becomes the interpretation, navigation, and reuse layer.

## Success condition

The wiki is doing its job if it lets the user understand current state, recent changes, open questions, and reusable language without reopening every project from scratch.
