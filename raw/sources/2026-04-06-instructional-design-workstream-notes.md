## Core idea

This workstream is a delivery-ready instructional design workbench.
It translates abstract capabilities into teachable structures, facilitator notes, slide systems, workbooks, and print assets.

## Main evidence

- the planning course reframes planning power as translating organizational expectation into executable work, not formatting documents
- the storytelling course reframes storytelling as moving people toward change and action, not as decorative speaking technique
- both guides are tightly operational and include timing, teaching intention, participant movement, and concrete outputs
- the folder structure indicates ongoing production of slides, scripts, worksheets, and renderable assets

## Key themes

- structure before polish
- action and transfer before performance
- facilitator intent made explicit in the guide, not left implicit
- course assets built as reusable delivery infrastructure

## Current limit

This ingest is based on two operating guides.
The slide decks, scripts, and workbook files likely contain more detailed evidence that has not yet been fully ingested into the wiki.

## Why this matters in the broader corpus

This workbench appears to be the execution engine behind both proposals and the broader teaching worldview:

- enterprise courses become reproducible
- facilitation logic is documented, not improvised
- teaching is treated as design work with artifacts
